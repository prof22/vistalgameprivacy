1. Introduction

Thank you for playing Word Vistas. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and what rights you have. By installing and playing Word Vistas, you agree to the practices described here.

Word Vistas is a free mobile game containing four mini-games: Word Search, Word Puzzle, Ball Sort, and Bubble Shooter. The app is supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Collect Directly

Data Type Details
Player name Optionally entered by you in Word Search for the leaderboard. Stored only on your device using SharedPreferences and never transmitted to our servers.
Game progress Level numbers, scores, high scores, and achievements. Stored on-device only.
Sound preference Whether you have muted the app. Stored on-device only.

We do not maintain any backend servers, user accounts, or cloud databases.

4. Children and COPPA / GDPR-K

Word Vistas is rated Everyone (E) and is designed for general audiences. It is not directed at children under 13 years old.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, please contact us at okoh.emmanuel.dev@gmail.com and we will take reasonable steps to address the request and remove applicable information where required.

For our AdMob integration, we have configured the app with a maxAdContentRating of T (Teen) or lower and have not tagged the app as directed to children under COPPA, as the content is not designed for that audience.

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Legal obligations — where disclosure is required by applicable law, court order, or a regulatory authority.

6. Data Retention

  • On-device data (game progress, player name, and settings) is retained until you uninstall the app or clear the app data through your Android settings.

7. Security

Game data is stored using Android's SharedPreferences mechanism, which is sandboxed to the app. No game data is transmitted to our servers because we do not operate backend servers for Word Vistas.

We take reasonable precautions to protect information processed locally, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
